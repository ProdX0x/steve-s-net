Privacy policy

GramGramTV 1.1 for Apple TV · Last updated: November 19, 2026 · Version française

In short

  • The developer receives nothing from the app. GramGramTV has no server: your messages, your searches, your files and any statistics are never sent to the developer.
  • Your conversations go directly between your Apple TV and Telegram, through Telegram's official library (TDLib).
  • TMDB receives the text of your video captions. To show a movie poster, the app sends TMDB (The Movie Database, United States) the caption of each video on screen, including in your private conversations, together with your Apple TV's IP address. Version 1.1 has no setting to prevent this (section 3.2).
  • YouTube (Google) receives the ID of YouTube videos linked in a message, to show their thumbnail.
  • No ads, no audience measurement and no tracking in the app. The developer does not sell any data.

1. Who is responsible, and how to reach him

GramGramTV is developed and published by Stéphane Saulnier (ProdX0x), in France, referred to here as "the developer". He is responsible for what the app does with your data.

  • Email: contact@steve-s.net

GramGramTV is an unofficial Telegram client: it uses the Telegram API, but it is not published or endorsed by Telegram.

2. What the app does, and what the developer receives

GramGramTV shows on your TV the conversations, groups and channels of your Telegram account: messages, photos, videos, audio and PDF documents. It can read PDFs aloud. It publishes nothing: it sends no messages, no reactions and no files.

The developer receives nothing from the app. It has no server, no audience measurement tool and no ads. Your data never goes through the developer.

He only receives information in two cases, outside the app:

  • You email him. He receives your address and your message. He uses them only to reply to you (legal basis: his legitimate interest in handling your request, GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)) and deletes them no later than 12 months after your last exchange.
  • You have agreed to share analytics with app developers in your Apple TV settings. Apple may then send him crash reports and usage statistics, aggregated or in a form that does not identify you (section 3.5).

3. What leaves your Apple TV, recipient by recipient

The companies below handle what they receive under their own privacy policies, which apply in addition to this one. The developer does not control them. For what they keep, contact them directly: their links are given in each section.

3.1 Telegram

Who. Telegram Messenger Inc. ("Telegram"), located outside the European Economic Area. The app uses TDLib, Telegram's official library, built into the app.

What is sent, and when.

  • As soon as the app opens, even if you do not sign in, and also in demo mode: a connection to Telegram's servers with the device type, the tvOS version, the app version and the Apple TV's language, followed by a request for a sign-in QR code.
  • When you sign in: you scan the QR code with Telegram on your phone. Telegram then opens a session linked to your account. You do not type your phone number in the app.
  • If your account has a password (two-step verification): you type it on the Apple TV. It is checked using Telegram's protocol (SRP): the app sends a proof computed from the password, not the password itself, and does not keep it.
  • While you browse: the requests needed to show your chats, your messages, senders' names and photos, and to download the photos, videos and files you open.
  • When you run a search: the text you typed or dictated.
  • When you sign out: the request to close the session.
  • Like any internet connection, these exchanges carry your Apple TV's IP address.

Why, and on what basis. To give you access to your Telegram account on your TV, which is what you ask for by using the app. Legal basis: performance of the app's terms of use (GDPR Art. 6(1)(b)). Telegram then processes this data on its own behalf, in particular on the basis of its legitimate interests, including security and preventing abuse, as described in its policy.

What Telegram keeps.

  • The session: it appears in the list of devices connected to your Telegram account, with the device model, the versions, the date of last activity, the last known IP address, and the country and region Telegram determines from it. It stays there until it is closed.
  • Security metadata, such as the IP address and devices used: "12 months maximum", according to Telegram.
  • Your searches: Telegram's policy does not say whether they are kept.
  • Your messages and files stay in Telegram's cloud, as with any Telegram app. GramGramTV adds nothing to them.
  • Telegram says it may use the IP address to tailor its ads to a country and city. GramGramTV 1.1 shows no Telegram ads.

Where. According to Telegram, the data of accounts opened from the European Economic Area is stored in data centers in the Netherlands. It may be shared with companies of its group located in the British Virgin Islands and in Dubai, under standard contractual clauses approved by the European Commission. Telegram has appointed EDPO (European Data Protection Office) as its representative in the Union.

Learn more, and exercise your rights with Telegram: https://telegram.org/privacy

3.2 TMDB (The Movie Database)

Who. TMDB, operated by TiVo Platform Technologies LLC, part of the Xperi group, with an address in San Jose, California (United States).

What is sent.

  • The text of the caption of each video on screen. The app removes the file extension, technical labels (such as "1080p" or "VOSTFR") and anything after a year, then sends the rest if it is at least two characters long, together with the year found.
  • In a channel's grid, the channel name, when the video has no caption.
  • The language "French" and the developer's TMDB access key, which is the same for all users.
  • Your Apple TV's IP address, as with any connection.
  • If TMDB finds a movie, the app downloads its poster from TMDB's image servers, with the same IP address.

The app adds no Telegram identifier: neither phone number nor account name. But a caption can contain any text, such as a first name or a place, and may have been written by someone other than you.

When. Automatically, as soon as a video appears on screen, without you opening it: in a conversation, private ones included, a group, a channel or search results. Only once per text while the app stays open.

Why, and on what basis. To show the movie poster when the caption gives its title. Legal basis: the developer's legitimate interest in illustrating videos (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)). You can object to it (section 7).

How to avoid it.

  • Version 1.1 has no setting that stops this. The "Privacy Shield" setting hides previews on screen, but does not prevent it.
  • The only way is not to display videos in the app, or not to use the app, until the next version.
  • The version in preparation no longer sends anything to TMDB. This page will be updated on the day it is released.

What TMDB keeps. Its policy says it may collect the IP address, URL information (the address called contains the searched text) and information related to searches, in particular to analyze the use of its services. It also lists, among what it may collect, general geolocation data from IP addresses. It keeps this information as long as necessary for its business purposes, without a stated period.

Where. TMDB says that data collected in the European Economic Area may be processed outside it, including in the United States. Its policy mentions no specific safeguard for these transfers. The developer uses TMDB under its standard terms of use, without clauses specific to this transfer.

Learn more, and exercise your rights with TMDB: https://www.themoviedb.org/privacy-policy · privacyrequest@xperi.com

This application uses TMDB and the TMDB APIs but is not endorsed, certified, or otherwise approved by TMDB.

3.3 YouTube (Google)

What is sent, and when. When a message in a conversation contains a YouTube link, the app shows the video's thumbnail by loading it from YouTube's servers, as soon as the message appears. Google then receives the video ID and your Apple TV's IP address.

Why, and on what basis. To show a preview of the link. Legal basis: the developer's legitimate interest in making links recognizable (GDPR Art. 6(1)(f)).

How to avoid it. No setting in version 1.1 stops this loading. The "Demander avant d'ouvrir YouTube" setting (ask before opening YouTube) only applies to opening the video.

Next. If you choose "Regarder sur YouTube" (watch on YouTube), the app opens the YouTube app: what happens there is up to YouTube.

Who, where, how long. For users in the European Economic Area and Switzerland, the controller is Google Ireland Limited. Google says it may process data on servers located outside your country. Retention: according to Google's policy, https://policies.google.com/privacy

3.4 Technical fallback services

If Telegram's servers cannot be reached, Telegram's library (TDLib) may query technical services to find their address: Google (public DNS and Firebase), Cloudflare and Microsoft Azure. These services see your Apple TV's IP address, but receive no data from your account.

3.5 Apple

  • App Store: downloading and updating the app go through Apple, under its policy: https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/
  • Dictation: if you dictate into a text field with the remote, tvOS captures and transcribes your voice; the app only receives the text. According to Apple, what you say is processed on the device or sent to Apple depending on your settings. Your audio is not stored by Apple unless you opted in to "Improve Siri and Dictation". Request history, tied to a random identifier, may be kept for up to two years. https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/ask-siri-dictation/
  • Reading PDFs aloud: text recognition, language detection and the voice are handled by features built into tvOS. GramGramTV sends this text to no one.
  • Analytics: if you turned on "Share With App Developers", Apple may send the developer crash data and usage statistics, aggregated or in a form that does not identify you. You can turn this sharing off in the Apple TV's privacy settings. https://www.apple.com/legal/privacy/data/en/app-analytics/

Apple, not the developer, is responsible for this processing.

5. What stays on your Apple TV, and how to erase it

What the app keeps on the Apple TV, in the app's own storage:

  • Telegram's database: your session and a copy of your chats and messages, to show them quickly;
  • downloaded photos, videos and files;
  • the "Reprendre la lecture" (resume playback) history: for the last 20 videos or PDFs, the title, a small thumbnail and where you stopped;
  • your settings (Privacy Shield, confirmation before YouTube) and the last section you opened;
  • only while the app is open, images in memory (posters, thumbnails).

The app does not keep your search history.

Good to know.

  • The app has no lock code: anyone using your Apple TV can open your conversations while you stay signed in.
  • The "Privacy Shield" setting replaces message preview text with "Aperçu masqué" (preview hidden) and hides or blurs media thumbnails, in lists and on the home screen. It does not hide conversation names, media captions, or the content of an open conversation.

How to erase it.

  • Sign out: in the app, Réglages › Compte Telegram › "Se déconnecter" (Settings › Telegram account › Sign out; an internet connection is required). The session is closed at Telegram and, according to TDLib's documentation, all its local data is destroyed. The resume-playback history and your settings remain on the Apple TV.
  • Delete the app: in the Apple TV's Settings, General › Manage Storage, then "Delete" next to GramGramTV. The app and all its data are erased. Do not choose "Offload": the app is removed but its data is kept. The "Offload Unused Apps" option also keeps the data.
  • Close the session from your phone, for example if you no longer have access to the Apple TV: in Telegram, open the list of connected devices (Settings › Devices, or Privacy & Security › Active Sessions) and terminate the Apple TV session. To also erase what remains on the Apple TV, delete the app.
  • Delete your Telegram account: https://telegram.org/deactivate. According to Telegram, this removes your messages, media and contacts from its cloud, and cannot be undone.

6. Demo mode

Demo mode, available from the sign-in screen, shows fictional content without a Telegram account. Even in demo mode:

  • the app connects to Telegram when it opens (section 3.1);
  • it sends TMDB the fictional titles of the demo videos ("Démo · …");
  • it loads from YouTube the thumbnail of a real YouTube video quoted in a fictional conversation.

A demo page says that "aucune donnée ne quitte l'appareil" (no data leaves the device): this is not accurate for version 1.1. This sentence will be corrected in the next version.

7. Your rights

Under the GDPR, you can request access to your data, its correction, its erasure, the restriction of its processing and its portability. You can also object, on grounds relating to your particular situation, to processing based on legitimate interest: sending captions to TMDB, loading YouTube thumbnails, keeping your emails.

In practice.

  • The developer holds no data from the app. He therefore cannot give you a copy of it, nor erase any at Telegram, TMDB, Google or Apple. He answers your questions and handles requests about your emails, at contact@steve-s.net, within one month, which may be extended by two months if the request is complex.
  • For what Telegram, TMDB, Google or Apple keep, contact them (links in section 3).
  • On the Apple TV, you erase the data yourself (section 5).
  • Objecting to sending data to TMDB or YouTube: version 1.1 cannot turn it off, and writing to the developer does not stop it on your Apple TV. The only immediate way is not to display videos or YouTube links, or not to use the app, until the next version.
  • The developer makes no automated decisions about you and does no profiling.
  • You are not required to provide any data. Without a Telegram account, only demo mode is available.
  • The developer handles requests the same way from every country where the app is offered.

Complaints. You can lodge a complaint with a data protection authority, in particular in the country where you live, where you work or where the issue occurred. In France: the CNIL, https://www.cnil.fr/en/home

8. Minimum age

GramGramTV requires a Telegram account, except in demo mode. Telegram's terms of service set a minimum age, 18 for users from European Union countries: https://telegram.org/tos. The developer receives no data from the app, including from a minor.
